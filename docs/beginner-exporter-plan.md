# A first market to research

Product proposal · 25 November 2026

**Give a first-time Indonesian exporter one useful outcome in five minutes.** They can finish the sentence “I can supply this product. I’ll investigate this country first, because ____. Before I spend money, I need to check ____.” Someone who is just curious can stop earlier and explain one change in the latest reported trade year.

Start with Indonesian small businesses that already make a product or can ask a supplier about it. That constraint gives the data a purpose. Someone without a product can explore a worked example and leave with a question about what they can source locally. Picking a product to invest in would require different evidence about supply, costs and buyers.

## What the current product already does

The [current brief](PRD.md) starts with an exporter who knows their six-digit product classification (HS6). An operator imports UN Comtrade data, calculates a ranking per product, and publishes a fixed version in Supabase. The website reads that version. Gemini can select findings in a later batch, and the site can explain the figures from templates on its own. The catalog has 12 product categories, ten candidate destinations and four annual periods, 2022–2025. The India solar pilot is a separate regression example. The working assumption is many readers sharing those releases and one operator refreshing them. [Release scope](../README.md) · [Publication architecture](architecture.md)

The following findings are ordered by their impact on a first visit. They come from tracing the routes and data, pending observation with beginners.

## What one visit should feel like

Use two entry points on mobile. **“Saya punya produk”** opens a product picker. **“Lihat contoh pasar”** opens a dated, worked brief immediately. Nobody needs an account or an HS code to start. Someone looking for ideas can also browse the supported categories by things they can source, such as coffee, cocoa products or clothing. Give each example its own destination comparison. Scores are relative to the destinations assessed for each individual product.

**A real example for the first prototype.** A coffee grower selects *kopi*, then confirms *biji kopi mentah, belum disangrai, bukan dekaf*. A seller who selects roasted coffee sees that this category is outside current coverage and can browse a clearly labelled example. Keep the exact product description and HS 090111 visible for checking.

| What the grower sees | Copy to test in Bahasa Indonesia |
| --- | --- |
| What happened most recently? | **Pada 2025, nilai impor biji kopi mentah Jerman naik 38% dibanding 2024, menjadi sekitar US$6,50 miliar.** Angka ini mencakup pembelian dari semua negara pemasok. |
| Why investigate Germany? | Dari 10 pasar yang diperiksa, 9 punya data yang bisa dibandingkan. Jerman berada di urutan pertama. Dari 2022 hingga 2025, pangsa Indonesia naik sekitar 1,68 poin persentase. Artinya, bagian Indonesia dari setiap US$100 yang diimpor Jerman bertambah sekitar US$1,68. |
| Why look at another market? | Tiongkok memiliki pasar yang lebih kecil, sekitar US$1,57 miliar pada 2025. Namun nilai impornya naik 87% dibanding 2024. Pilih Jerman untuk meneliti pasar yang lebih besar, atau Tiongkok untuk meneliti kenaikan nilai impor yang lebih cepat. |
| What is still unknown? | Nilai impor bisa naik karena harga, jumlah barang, atau keduanya. Dari angka ini saja, kita belum tahu mutu kopi yang dicari pembeli atau berapa yang bersedia mereka bayar. |
| What can I do this week? | **Siapkan spesifikasi satu lot kopi.** Tanyakan kepada pemasok jenis biji, proses pengolahan, jumlah yang tersedia, dan kapan sampel bisa dikirim. Bawa catatan itu saat meminta masukan dari pendamping ekspor tentang pasar pilihan Anda. |

These figures come from the public coffee snapshot published 24 November 2026 UTC, ending in trade year 2025. Both one-year changes use 2024 as the base. Germany’s 15.7% and China’s 45.9% annualized growth over 2022–2025 are different measures used in the ranking. A reviewer can reproduce the example at `/rankings/090111/276` and `/rankings/090111/156` with the query `?snapshot=adeafe3bcd853d59cbf52202e620a6d6fe3bfea187112d46929d0ba50cc8ec9e`. Source for the underlying trade statistics: [UN Comtrade](https://comtradeplus.un.org/).

## Give Gemini a decision worth making

Keep the numeric ranking reproducible. The existing method gives equal weight to destination import value, three-year import-value growth and the change in Indonesia’s share, compared with eligible markets for that product. Display it under **“Mengapa pasar ini muncul?”** A second overall score for exporter readiness would require inputs the site cannot observe.

Give Gemini a narrower, testable job. **Decide which evidence and counterpoint a beginner should see first, then choose the research question that follows.** For example, Australia and the US tied on the published coffee shortlist. Indonesia gained share in Australia and lost share in the US. A model that simply calls both “promising” has missed the useful distinction. A useful US brief leads with market size and surfaces the share loss, then invites the reader to investigate which suppliers gained ground in the same period.

| Layer | Proposed decision |
| --- | --- |
| Analytics | Compute observed direction, period, comparable market count, score components and coverage from the pinned snapshot. Keep latest-year change distinct from the multi-year ranking. A relative winner can still have falling import value. |
| Gemini | Receive anonymous slots and approved derived signal categories. Return IDs for a lead finding, a qualifying fact, and a question from a small reviewed set. Example question choices include “Which suppliers gained share?” and “What do I know about the product I can supply?” |
| Validation | In TypeScript, check each ID against the allowed facts and questions **for that pair and period**. Require a contrary fact when signals disagree. If data is insufficient, show the specific gap. A missing supplier row cannot support “Indonesia supplied zero.” Limited weight coverage affects comparisons by weight. |
| Reader | Render reviewed Indonesian sentences with numbers, units, years and source inserted locally. Show one next action. For a person who has a supplier, offer a specification check. For someone browsing, offer a worked comparison. This last choice can happen on the device. |
| Operations | Extend the existing batch, version the rubric and templates, and cache by the evidence and generation version. Keep source data and personal answers out of the model request. If validation fails, quota runs out, or a visitor changes years, show the deterministic brief for the relevant evidence. |

**Make Gemini earn its place.** Compare it against a simple rule that picks the strongest fact and any opposing signal. Blind the labels when testing. Score each brief on factual support, whether it reveals the trade-off and whether the reader chooses a relevant next check. If the rule performs as well, ship the rule for that case. Spend model calls where choosing among several supported explanations improves understanding.

## Build sequence

| Step | Build or learn | Acceptance before moving on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Observe the existing journey | Recruit five Indonesian first-time exporters, including a producer with a known product and someone exploring options. On their phones, ask them to pick a market using the current site. Record the words they search for, time to a choice, what they think the score means and when they ask for help. | Capture a baseline and three observed points of confusion. Use the participants’ own terms in the prototype. |
| 2. Prototype one complete coffee visit | Use the verified example above in Bahasa Indonesia. Reuse the current visual style. Put the product and latest year first, two contrasting markets second, and the next action third. Put definitions next to the terms and use an expandable section for calculations. | A participant can get from “kopi” to the correct product, read the brief and name a task. A roasted-coffee seller can recognize the coverage gap. Revise the copy before coding additional categories. |
| 3. Ship the deterministic version | Add a small reviewed name-and-form lookup for coffee and preserve access to all existing rankings. Reuse the published ranking, market detail and pinned links. Use the same snapshot for both sides of a comparison. Put the latest-year brief on the example path. Keep the source and years visible at phone width. | Check ties, declining markets, missing Indonesian share, one eligible market, no publication, reader failure and old observations in a newly published release. Show a useful explanation for each. Verify keyboard flow, chart text equivalents and the primary action at 320px. |
| 4. Add Gemini in the operator batch | Extend the finding schema and generation version. Build a small evaluation set including Germany versus China, the Australia–US tie, mixed directions, missing years and limited weight coverage. Have an Indonesian-speaking export adviser review the facts, language and action choices. | Every displayed factual statement traces to the selected snapshot and period. Reject unsupported IDs and exercise timeout, invalid output and cache-miss paths. In a blinded comparison with the template, enable Gemini for cases where readers better explain the trade-off without a loss of accuracy. |
| 5. Pilot the action and test return value | Offer a short product-specific task and an appropriate handoff. A coffee grower can prepare a lot specification. A business with that sheet can check which destination requirements need confirmation using Kemendag’s EXIM resources. A user seeking supplier visibility can review Inaexport’s registration conditions. Ask after a week what they actually did. | Proposed first gate: four of five new testers can name the trade year, distinguish import value from sales prospects, and choose a sensible next step unaided in five minutes. At least three can describe a concrete follow-up they completed within a week. Treat these small counts as a signal for another round. [Kemendag EXIM](https://exim.kemendag.go.id/) · [Inaexport](https://www.inaexport.id/about) |
| 6. Expand where the pilot shows demand | Add the next two catalog products that participants can actually supply, with reviewed aliases, product-form distinctions and one realistic action each. For a recurring “what changed?” visit, publish dated briefs when a new comparable period arrives. Test a separate monthly series only if people need newer decisions and source coverage supports it. | Each added product passes the same comprehension test. For any higher-frequency series, display coverage and compare matching periods. Retain annual ranking methodology until a separate method is validated. Pursue watchlists or alerts once repeat visits demonstrate a reason to subscribe. |

**Scope decision for the next release.** The current PRD makes Bahasa Indonesia optional and assumes an exporter who already knows their product classification. The proposed pilot makes Indonesian the default for the beginner journey and adds guided product identification and a concrete research task. Adopt those changes in the PRD if the prototype succeeds. The existing analyst view, annual score and operator-published snapshots can support the first release. Buyer matching, individualized compliance determinations and margin forecasts would each need additional evidence and a separate product decision.
